About AFAB Nation

AFAB means Assigned Female at Birth.

AFAB Nation is an independent female-centered social, music and media platform built for women to talk, create, laugh, debate, share and be heard.

We're part social network, part independent radio station, part satire machine, and part women's community.

What We Believe

AFAB Nation's editorial position is simple:

Sex is binary: female and male. Biological sex cannot be changed.

Being a woman does not require dresses, makeup, femininity, motherhood, long hair, heels or any particular personality.

Women can be feminine. Women can be masculine. Women can be somewhere in between.

Female is not a stereotype.

We believe women are entitled to advocate for privacy, safety, fairness, female sports and women's spaces, while still treating other people with basic human dignity.

AFAB Nation Radio

Our original music turns serious cultural arguments into something louder, funnier and harder to ignore.

Expect rock, soul, jazz, funk, Latin, alternative, dance, Broadway, comedy and unapologetic satire.

Some songs make a serious point. Some make you think. Some are completely ridiculous.

That's the point.

Speak. Create. Post.

AFAB Nation is being built as more than a website.

Women can share text, images, audio and music, participate in conversations and discover independent creators.

We want a platform where women aren't expected to whisper their opinions because somebody on the internet might object.

Free expression does not mean harassment. Community rules still apply.

AFAB + AMAB

AFAB Nation is the first half of a larger idea.

AFAB Nation = Assigned Female at Birth
AMAB Nation = Assigned Male at Birth

Men deserve spaces to discuss men's lives and issues too.

AMAB Nation is being developed separately as AFAB Nation's companion platform, using the same basic technology while maintaining its own community, identity, music and spaces.

Independent by Design

AFAB Nation is being bootstrapped independently.

Free radio, premium music, merch, community support and sponsors help build an ecosystem capable of supporting itself without turning every feature into another monthly subscription.
